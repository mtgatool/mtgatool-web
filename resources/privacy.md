# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 4th, 2026*

This policy explains what data MTG Arena Tool ("the application") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers the desktop application and this website.

We are an independent, community project and are not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.

## Information we collect

**Your MTG Arena account.** To separate your game data by player, the application reads your MTG Arena account identifier and screen name from the game's own output logs.

**Your game activity.** While the application is running alongside MTG Arena, it records events from those logs. This may include match results and opponents, decklists, draft picks, your card collection, inventory and rank progress.

**Your MTG Arena Tool profile.** The display name and avatar you choose for your account.

**Account credentials.** A password, and optionally a recovery email address if you choose to add one so that you can reset that password. Adding a recovery email is not required to use the application.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, payment details, or any information about you outside of MTG Arena.

## How we use it

We use this information to provide the application: to store your history, to sync it between your devices, and to power features such as statistics, deck performance and collection tracking.

Aggregated and anonymised game data — for example, which decks and cards are being played — is used to build community features such as Explore.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or other third parties for their own purposes. We use service providers to host our infrastructure, and they process data only on our behalf in order to run the service.

## What other people can see

Some features show your profile publicly to other users, such as your display name, avatar and rank.

**Private mode** turns this off. Enable it under **Settings → Account**, and your profile will not be shown to other users.

## Your choices

- **Private mode.** Under **Settings → Account**, to keep your profile from being shown to other users.
- **Recovery email.** Optional, and can be added or changed under **Settings → Account**.
- **Deleting matches.** Individual matches can be removed from your history inside the application.
- **Deleting your account.** Contact us at the address below and we will delete your account and the data associated with it.

You can also use the application without signing in. In that case your data stays on your device and is not sent to us, though it will not sync between devices.

## Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove it and the data associated with it.

## Children

The application is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the application after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests concerning your data, can be sent to [mtgatool@gmail.com](mailto:mtgatool@gmail.com), or raised on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/K9bPkJy).
